Program 02 · For what Florida ruins

Climate-controlled units

Florida humidity is what damages stored goods, more than heat. Upholstery, timber furniture, photographs, paperwork, electronics and anything with adhesive will all suffer in an unconditioned unit over a summer. We will tell you honestly when you do not need climate control, too.

Program 04 · Outdoor spaces

Vehicle, boat & trailer parking

Outdoor parking for things that will not fit on a driveway or that an HOA will not allow. Vehicles must be registered, insured and in running order, and nothing may leak — those are conditions of the space rather than preferences, and they protect everyone else parked there.
